You’re invited to test Shuck it

Shuck it is an iPhone app for finding seafood shacks worth the trip. It’s not in the App Store yet, and I’d love you to try it first and tell me what you think.

Get the test app

Opens TestFlight, Apple’s app for trying apps before they launch. If you don’t have it yet, the link shows you how to get it. Needs an iPhone on iOS 18 or later.

Things to know

Yes, it’s bold

The bright yellow, the chunky icons and the big type are on purpose. I wanted it to feel like a day at the seaside, not another grey map app. Tell me if it works for you or if it’s too much.

London and the South East, for now

There are about 100 places so far, mostly in London and along the coast from Kent to Sussex. If you’re further afield the map will look a bit empty. More areas will follow if it’s worth carrying on.

Photos come from Google, so some won’t load

Google charges for its photos, so I’ve capped how many the app can show each day. If you see “No image”, that’s why, not a bug. Everything else, like descriptions, opening hours and which places are on the map, comes from an AI research agent I built. I review what it finds each week.

What is a seafood shack, exactly?

I’m still working that out. A hut on a harbour, sure, but what about a fishmonger with a few stools, or an oyster bar? For now the map shows shacks, oyster bars and seafood restaurants, and Filters lets you choose which ones you see.

The ratings are a starting point

Each place starts with my own rating so the map isn’t empty. Your ratings move it up or down. There are no Google reviews in the app.

You don’t need an account to look around

Browse the map, list and search signed out. Sign in with Apple or Google to like, save, check off and rate places, and to use Filters. You can delete your account at any time in Settings.

Know a place that’s missing?

Go to Account, tap Submit a shack and paste a Google Maps link. I read every one.

It’s a test version

Things will break, and some details will be wrong. TestFlight will offer updates as I fix things, so keep it updated.

Feedback I’m looking for

Anything and everything, honestly. Short answers are great. These are the things I’m most unsure about:

  1. Is it any good?

    Would you actually use it to plan a day out? Is it worth putting on the App Store and spending a little money so the photos always load?

  2. Filters

    Are they useful? What else would you want to filter by? Open now, distance, outdoor seating, dog friendly, something else?

  3. Catch of the day

    The tab at the bottom of the map. Are its groups (Top spots, Shuck it faves, Open now, Top rated) useful, or is it worth having that tab at all?

  4. Did you ever get lost?

    Any moment you weren’t sure where you were, how to get back, or what a button did.

  5. Anything wrong or missing?

    Wrong opening hours, a place that’s closed, a pin in the wrong spot, something that isn’t really a seafood place, or a favourite of yours that isn’t there.

  6. The look

    First impressions of the colours, icons and type. Does it feel like a seafood app?

  7. The one thing

    If you could change one thing about Shuck it, what would it be?
