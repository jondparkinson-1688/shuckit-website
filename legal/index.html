Privacy Policy

Shuck it is an iPhone app for finding seafood shacks around the UK. It's made by Jonathan Parkinson, a sole trader in the UK, who is responsible for your information (the "data controller"). If you have any questions, email jondparkinson@gmail.com.

The short version: we collect as little as we can, we never sell it, and there are no ads, tracking or analytics in Shuck it.

You don't need an account to browse

You can look at the map, the list, search and every place's details without signing in. You only need an account to like, save, check off or rate a place, or to use filters.

What we keep when you have an account

  • Who you are when you sign in. You sign in with Apple or Google; we never see a password. We receive an ID for your account and your email address. With Apple you can choose to hide your email, and Apple then gives us a private relay address instead. We don't ask Apple for your name.
  • Your places. The places you like, save and check off, with the date you did it, and the star ratings you give.

If you sign in with Google, your Google profile photo is shown in the app. It's loaded from Google each time and we don't keep a copy.

What stays on your phone

  • Your location. Only if you tap Locate me and allow it. It's used on your phone to centre the map on you and work out how far away places are. We never receive or store it.
  • Places you've viewed, your filter choices, and a copy of the places so the map loads quickly.

Why we use it

We use your information only to run Shuck it for you: keeping you signed in, showing your liked, saved and checked places on any phone you sign in on, and adding your ratings to each place's score. The legal basis is that it's needed to provide the app you've asked to use. We don't use it for advertising and we don't sell or share it for marketing.

Other companies involved

  • Supabase stores accounts and your places, on servers in Ireland (EU).
  • Apple and Google handle signing in.
  • Google Maps provides the map, and some opening hours and photos. Your phone asks Google for these directly, so Google receives the usual technical details (such as your IP address) under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple helps find towns and areas when you search. What you type in Search is sent to Apple for this, under Apple's Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Account › Settings › Delete account). When you do:

  • your account, and the places you liked, saved and checked off, are deleted;
  • ratings of 1 or 2 stars are deleted;
  • ratings of 3 stars or more are kept, but with nothing linking them to you (not even to each other), so places keep a fair score.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it or delete it, object to how it's used, or ask for it in a portable format. Email jondparkinson@gmail.com and we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Shuck it isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep information about them.

Check before you go

We do our best to keep places, opening hours and other details accurate, but shacks change their hours, close for the season or shut for good, often without notice. Some details come from Google. Please check with the place before making a special trip. Shuck it isn't responsible for a wasted journey.

Ratings

Rate places honestly, based on your own visits. Don't rate a place you're connected to, and don't try to push a score up or down. We may remove ratings or close accounts that break these rules.
